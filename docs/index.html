The bargain, split honestly

Two paths. One goal.

Allowance apps either gamify chores with fake money or hand kids a debit card. Do My Chore splits the bargain instead.

The kid's path is habit

Earn 100% of the goal

Every chore has a cadence and a weight. "Make your bed" daily is worth 40% of the goal, paid out one tiny slice per check-in. Weights sum to at least 100% - parents can deliberately oversubscribe so a few skipped days don't sink the plan.

  • Percent only. No dollars, coins, or store on any kid screen.
  • Photo check-ins for chores a parent can verify by looking.
  • A rejected chore is a next try - a kind note from the parent, never a failure.
  • Finish the day and get the moment: That's today done.
The parent's path is the promise

Plan the real cost

The same app is a financial planner. Disneyland is $3,500 over 14 weeks - so the parent saves about $250 a week.

  • Dollars live here. Save cadence, saved-so-far, and an honest catch-up rate if you slip.
  • A manual I saved this week log keeps the promise visible.
  • Approve or send back with a note - previewing exactly what the kid will read.
  • Makeup chores exist if the kid falls behind - off by default, never automatic.
How it works

From goal to earned, in four moves

1

Set the goal

Family trip or kid item, real cost, target date. The planner does the save math - about $250 a week for a $3,500 trip.

2

Suggest the plan

AI drafts a weekly save plus weighted habits that sum to at least 100%. Edit any line, then accept. It explains its why in plain parent language.

3

The kid checks in

Photo proof where looking works, trust where it doesn't. Each approval moves the bar a tiny slice of the weight.

4

100% earns it

The bar fills, the day gets its moment, and the finale hands off to the parent - the trip is talked about together, tonight.

AI where it helps, fenced where it must be

AI that suggests. Parents decide.

Both AI moments run deterministically with zero API keys - the demo never depends on a key being set.

Suggest plan

Given the goal, cost, date, and the kid's age, the AI proposes a weekly save and 4-8 weighted chores summing to at least 100% - with a why in plain parent language.

The fence: weights are validated against the sum rule; photos only on visually verifiable chores; no API key means the deterministic builder produces the same shape.

Photo assist

For chores a parent can verify by looking, it suggests approve or send back. Trust-based chores - reading time - never ask for photos.

The fence: the parent is always final. The app says looks complete - you make the call, and means it. No verification claims.
The Goal Album

Approved check-in photos collect into a deletable album of the goal you're building toward. Demo photos are test data. Check-in photos stay in your family's scoped storage.

Honesty first

No bank. No card.
No fake promises.

The app plans money and tracks habits. Real money moves between parent and kid, offline. That honesty is a feature, not a limitation - and it's fenced in the code.

What we never claim

  • No banking, card, or KYC features - ever. Kid screens show percent only.
  • No verification-accuracy claims. The AI suggests; the parent decides.
  • No kids'-privacy compliance claims. Demo photos are labeled test data.
  • Family data stays family data: row-level security on every table, storage paths scoped by family.
